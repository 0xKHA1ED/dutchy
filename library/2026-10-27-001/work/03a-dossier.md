# Case File: Khadija Amrani

## 1. Identity

**Name:** Khadija Amrani. Like most Moroccan women of her generation, she kept her own family name after marriage. Colleagues call her "Lalla Khadija". The manager calls her "Hajja", although she has never made the pilgrimage.

**Age:** 63. Born 2 March 1962. Her last night shift is in 21 days.

**Residence:** A third-floor apartment in a 1970s block in the ville nouvelle of Fès, off Boulevard Mohammed V. She lives with her husband, Youssef Benjelloun, 68. Since their son moved out in 2011, they have slept in separate rooms.

**Appearance:**
- She is 1.58 m and broad in the hips.
- Her shoulders slope downward from the neck, the left lower than the right.
- She walks with a slight hitch from a stiff left hip, so her left foot lands flatter than her right.
- Her hair is dyed with henna, auburn at the ends and grey at the parting. It is always under a scarf, tied at the nape, never under the chin.
- She has a gold front-left molar that shows only when she laughs properly.

**Dress:**
- For work she wears the store's navy tabard over a long grey skirt and a cardigan.
- Off duty she wears a djellaba, beige or bottle-green. She owns four and rotates them by day of the week.
- She owns no garment that leaves the shoulders bare, including nightwear.
- On her feet she wears black Velcro orthopaedic sandals, size 39, bought at a pharmacy.

## 2. Origins

She was born in Fès el-Bali, in a rented house off Talaa Kebira with a courtyard and one fig tree in a tub. Her father, Mohamed Amrani, sold buttons, thread and zips from a stall in the Kissaria. Her mother, Zohra (née Bouzid), embroidered *terz fassi* for brides' trousseaux and was paid by the piece. She was always two months behind on the orders.

**Siblings:**
- Abdelkader, born 1959. He went to Lyon in 1979 and drove forklifts. He now lives in Villeurbanne and visits every second August.
- Khadija, born 1962.
- Zohra, born 1967, named after the mother. She still lives in the medina, two streets from the old house.

**An ordinary weekday evening, c. 1974:** The father came home after the *maghrib* prayer and sat with the radio, which played football commentary or Koran depending on the night. The mother embroidered under a single bulb. Khadija's job was threading needles for her mother, six at a time, laid on a cushion. Dinner was eaten from one dish, in silence except for the radio.

**Never discussed at dinner:**
- The mother's younger sister, who went to Brussels in 1970, divorced there and stopped writing. Her name was not said in the house. A framed photograph of three girls on the mother's dresser had one face covered by a folded square of card.
- The father's debt to a cloth wholesaler named Haj Belkacem, which lasted from 1971 to 1980.

## 3. Three formative scenes

**A. The back game, summer 1976 (age 14).**
Every Saturday Khadija and her neighbour Fatiha Kaddour went to the neighbourhood hammam with their mothers. The mothers gossiped in the warm room. In the hot room the girls scrubbed each other's backs with the black soap and the rough glove. Fatiha pushed her sleeves up past the elbow before starting, though she was naked and had no sleeves. When the scrubbing was done, Fatiha traced letters on Khadija's wet back with one finger, and Khadija had to guess the word. That August Fatiha wrote T-A-N-J-A, the Arabic name for Tangier. Khadija guessed "Taza" twice. Fatiha laughed so hard she slipped on the tiles and cut her chin on a bucket rim. She had a small white scar there afterwards, under the jaw.

**B. The gare routière, night of 14 October 1981 (age 19).**
That afternoon the two girls had gone to the night market at Bab Ftouh. By the time they got there the stalls were lit by carbide lamps. They bought two identical pairs of cheap black flat shoes with a gold buckle. The seller had only a 37 left for Khadija, who wore 38. "They'll stretch," Fatiha said. Fatiha had a 38. The bus to Tangier left at 23:30. At 23:10 Khadija stood across the road from the bus station under the awning of a closed café, holding her shoebox and a cloth bag. Through the bus window she could see the back of Fatiha's head, and the bag Fatiha had put on the seat beside her. The bus left at 23:42. Khadija walked home, about forty minutes. She put the shoebox under her bed and was lying down before the call to the dawn prayer. Nobody in the house had noticed she had left.

**C. The till, 3:10 a.m., 9 February 2014 (age 51).**
Two women came in together. One pushed a trolley with a single bottle of water in it. The other stood at Khadija's till asking the price of phone cards, one after another. Khadija scanned and answered and watched the first woman's hand. It went into the coat pocket of a sleepy taxi driver who was paying at the next till. Khadija did not look up. She said loudly, "Sir, your wallet is in the lady's left sleeve," and kept scanning. The security guard held the women until the police arrived. The regional manager later gave her a framed certificate, "Vigilance Award, Al Wifaq Group". She hung it in the store's staff toilet, at eye level, opposite the sink.

## 4. Work, money and status

**Role:** Cashier at Supermarché Al Wifaq, a 24-hour supermarket near the railway station. She started in 1996 on days and moved to nights in March 1998. Her shift runs 22:00–06:00, Sunday to Thursday.

**Competence:**
- She is the fastest scanner on the staff by the store's own figures.
- She knows all 140 produce codes by heart.
- She does change in her head before the till displays it.
- In 27 years her till has been short at closing twice: 20 dirhams in 2003 and 5 dirhams in 2017.
- She recognises regular night customers by their hands. Names and faces she remembers less well.

**Pay:** Base salary of 3,650 dirhams net, plus a night premium, comes to about 4,150 a month. She tells her sister Zohra she earns "around five thousand". She tells her son "enough". Her CNSS pension estimate, received in June, is 2,240 dirhams a month.

**Reporting:** She reports to the store manager, Hamid Mansouri, 34. Nobody reports to her, but new night cashiers are placed at the till next to hers "to learn".

**At the leaving party, the security guard Rachid would say:** "She never once let me go hungry, and she never once let me finish a sentence."

## 5. Love and sex

**Before marriage:** In 1980 she spoke three times to a boy named Tarek who sold cassettes at Bab Ftouh. When her colleague Naïma asked her in 2019 who her first love was, this is the name she gave.

**Marriage:** Her maternal aunt arranged it. She married Youssef Benjelloun, an Arabic teacher at a public collège, in July 1986. Their son Amine was born in 1988, after a first pregnancy was lost at eleven weeks in 1987.
- Sex was regular in the first years.
- It became infrequent after Amine's birth and stopped around the time she moved to nights in 1998.
- Neither has mentioned this since.
- Youssef has never raised his voice to her. He leaves a thermos of mint tea on the kitchen table every morning at 6:15, before her shift ends.

**Other men:** None.

**Fatiha:** From 1974 to 1981 the two girls slept on the same mattress on Khadija's roof on summer nights, with Zohra on a second mattress. They shared one lipstick, kept at Fatiha's house.

**Attraction:** She finds attractive a person who pushes their sleeves up past the elbow before starting a task. She will watch a man doing this in a shop or a garage longer than she watches anything else.

## 6. Body and health

**Conditions:**
- Osteoarthritis of the left hip, diagnosed 2016. It is worse on the side she pivots towards to push goods to the bagging area.
- Mild hypertension, treated with amlodipine 5 mg.
- Varicose veins in both calves.
- Her feet have widened to a 39.

**Physiotherapy:** In 2021 a physiotherapist photographed her back with his phone to show her the tilt of her shoulders and hips. She asked him to delete the picture and watched him do it.

**Sleep:** 07:30 to 13:30, with the curtains pinned shut with clothes pegs. She sleeps on her right side only.

**Food:**
- She eats harira from a flask at 02:00, and bread with olive oil and *amlou* at home.
- She does not eat sardines at all, though the rest of the family does.
- She buys three sachets of sunflower seeds a week and cracks them with her front teeth.

**Hands:** She folds every receipt, ticket and scrap of paper in four, corner to corner, then presses the fold with her thumbnail. She also does this with paper napkins at weddings.

**Self-conscious about:** Her back and shoulders. In cafés she sits with her back to a wall. There is no full-length mirror in the apartment. The bathroom mirror shows her from the collarbones up.

**Bathing:** She has not been to a public hammam since October 1981. She washes at home with a bucket and a plastic cup, and a hand-held shower installed in 2009.

## 7. Their space

**The apartment:** Three rooms, a kitchen and a bathroom.
- **Salon:** Moroccan banquettes in green velvet. The television stays on mute when Youssef is out.
- **Youssef's room:** The old marital bedroom. Crossword books are stacked by year.
- **Khadija's room:** Amine's old room, still with his football pennant (Maghreb de Fès) on the wall. She has not taken it down.

**On the fridge:**
- Her shift rota.
- A crayon drawing by her granddaughter Leïla of a woman with a very large handbag.
- A magnet from Morocco Mall, Casablanca.
- A CNSS appointment slip, folded in four and then unfolded, with the creases visible.

**Under her bed:** A shoebox wrapped in a supermarket bag. Inside, in yellowed tissue, are black flat shoes with a gold buckle, size 37, the soles unmarked. Also in the box is a CTM ticket, Fès–Tangier, 14/10/1981, 23:30, seat 22.

**The drawer she never opens:** The bottom drawer of her bedside table. It holds a Nido milk tin with no lid, empty, and a plastic hair clip shaped like a green butterfly that belonged to Fatiha.

**Car:** A white 2006 Dacia Logan, registered to Youssef and driven by her. In it:
- A jar of coins for parking.
- A hand towel.
- A sachet of sunflower seeds.
- A rosary hanging from the mirror, given by Zohra.
- A pair of fleece slippers she changes into to drive.

**Handbag:** Brown imitation leather. In it:
- A purse with exactly 200 dirhams in it at all times.
- Her pill box.
- A small notebook of night customers' "debts" of 1 or 2 dirhams. She has never collected on any of them.

**Phone:** A Samsung Galaxy A12.
- **Last five searches:** "calcul pension CNSS"; "élargir chaussures cuir trop petites"; "hammam femmes Bab Boujloud horaires"; "horodateur Bab Boujloud tarif"; "CTM Fès Tanger horaires nuit".
- **Most-used app:** WhatsApp. Second is a solitaire game: 4,112 games played.
- **Unread:**
  - 63 messages in the group "Famille Amrani".
  - A 2-minute voice note from Amine.
  - A message from Hamid: "Hajja!! Pot de départ le 28, gâteau commandé 😊".

## 8. A typical Tuesday

- **06:00** Hands over her till. Counts the drawer twice.
- **06:20** Drives home, slippers on.
- **06:40** Arrives. Youssef is at the table with the newspaper. Tea from the thermos. They discuss the price of tomatoes and his blood test results.
- **07:15** Youssef leaves for the café. She washes the glasses.
- **07:30–13:30** Sleeps.
- **13:45** Lunch: lentils, bread, yesterday's tajine if there is any. Youssef eats with her and does his crossword.
- **14:30** Errands: the pharmacy, the bakery, sometimes the bank to check the balance in person.
- **16:00** Calls Zohra and listens for 20 minutes.
- **17:00** Solitaire on the banquette, television muted.
- **18:30** Naps for one hour.
- **20:00** Light supper. Fills the harira flask.
- **21:20** Drives to work.
- **22:00** Shift starts.
- **02:00** Harira in the stockroom with Rachid.
- **03:00–05:00** The quiet hours: taxi drivers, nurses, students, the occasional drunk tourist. She comments under her breath on everything they buy.

## 9. Pleasures and competence

**Pleasures:**
- The 3 a.m. lull, when she can see the whole shop floor from her till.
- Cracking sunflower seeds.
- Egyptian black-and-white films on satellite channels, which she knows well enough to say the lines a second before the actors do.
- Her granddaughter's voice notes.

**Good at:**
- Mental arithmetic.
- Spotting theft.
- Threading needles without glasses, still.
- Embroidery: she can do *terz fassi* but has not since 1986.
- Imitations. She does the manager, the regular drunk, and the CNSS clerk. Her impression of Hamid saying "dynamique" makes Naïma cry with laughter.

**Makes her laugh:**
- People who try to scan a melon as an onion.
- Slapstick.
- Her own impressions.

**Proud of and never mentions:**
- She paid for Amine's two years of private BTS school by herself, from the night premium.
- In 2008 she taught Rachid's illiterate mother to sign her name in the stockroom, over six weeks of 2 a.m. breaks.

## 10. People

- **Youssef Benjelloun, 68, husband.** Their last real conversation, in August, was about whether to sell the Dacia when she retires. He said, "You'll be home now," and she went to wash the glasses. *She would not volunteer:* she has asked Hamid twice whether the store needs part-time night cover after retirement.

- **Amine Benjelloun, 37, son.** Works at a French telecom call centre in Casablanca. He is married to Meriem, with one daughter, Leïla, aged 6. His last call was a Sunday: he asked if she wanted to come and live with them. *She would not volunteer:* she has not opened his last voice note, which arrived nine days ago.

- **Zohra Amrani, 58, younger sister.** A seamstress in the medina. *She would not volunteer:* Zohra was on the second mattress on the roof in 1981 and knows the shoebox exists. Neither has ever mentioned it.

- **Naïma Haddad, 44, fellow night cashier.** She works the till beside Khadija's and is divorced, with twin boys. She bought Khadija's farewell gift: a voucher for the hammam near Bab Boujloud, women's hours, with a scrub by the attendant included. *She would not volunteer:* she said thank you, put it in her handbag, and went into the staff toilet for six minutes.

- **Rachid Belkacem, 29, night security guard.** He shares the 02:00 harira. He calls her "mmi", mum. Their last real conversation was about whether he should marry a girl from Sefrou. Khadija said: "If you're going, go."

- **Hamid Mansouri, 34, store manager.** He is unbearable to her. He says "dynamique" and "process", calls her "Hajja", and once moved her till half a metre to the left "for flow". *She would not volunteer:* she has kept every one of his memos, folded in four, in a carrier bag in her locker.

- **Mohamed Kaddour, about 58, Fatiha's younger brother.** He has a phone-repair stall in the medina near Bab Boujloud. *She would not volunteer:* for 30 years she has taken the long way round to avoid his street.

- **Fatiha Kaddour, 63.** Last seen 14 October 1981, 23:42, from behind, through a bus window. In November 1981 Fatiha twice telephoned the grocer on the corner, who had the only phone in the street, and asked for Khadija. Both times Khadija sent Zohra down to say she was out.

## 11. The secret

The two girls saved for their departure in a Nido tin kept at Khadija's house. When the bus left, the tin held 380 dirhams, of which 190 were Fatiha's. Khadija never returned the money. In June 1986 she spent the full 380, with 120 dirhams more, on a pair of small gold hoop earrings for her wedding. She has worn them every day since.

## 12. Recent events

- **June:** The CNSS pension letter arrived.
- **Late summer:** She took the shoebox out for the first time in years and tried the shoes on. Her toes did not reach the front. She put them back in the tissue.
- **Spring:** The municipality installed coin parking meters outside Bab Boujloud. She has complained about them to Youssef, to Zohra and to three customers.
- **Five weeks ago, 2:40 a.m.:** Mohamed Kaddour came through her till buying a phone charger and a Coca-Cola. He looked at her and said, "Khadija? Fatiha's friend?" She said, "Twelve dirhams fifty." At the door he turned and said that Fatiha "comes back for the summer now, her knees are bad, she likes the hammam." Khadija scanned the next customer's bread twice.
- **Three weeks to go:** Naïma gave her the hammam voucher, which expires at the end of the month.
- **Yesterday:** She did the "élargir chaussures" search on her phone.
