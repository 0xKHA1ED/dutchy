## Fixes

1. **Dija double too loud in 2.1.** Spread the cluster: move Fatiha's "Dija" out of 2.1 and into 2.4.
   - 2.1 keeps "Venga, the other shoulder. I'm not paid today…" without the name.

   - This leaves 2.1 with henna, shoulder and 39, gives 2.2 the museum echo, and lets the name land at the end of the chapter, after the comedy has cracked. The 3.3 "two women answer" keeps its force, and the itch-then-doubt progression the plan intends is restored.

   - Combined with "half" and the name *Fatiha* from 3.3, the full theft becomes reconstructable from the clues.

3. **Ledger and map claims not supported by what is planted.**
   - **The glove:** in the reframe map and the 4.4 ledger, change "the glove is Fatiha's" / "Fatiha's glove would have caught the thief" to "any glove on her back would have caught the thief." In 2.3 the glove is Souad's.
