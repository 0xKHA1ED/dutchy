## IFS reading of Souad Ben Salah

### Reading
Souad's system is organised around one fierce act of letting go, and a part that is quietly fighting it.

- **The Keeper** (about 40, the age she was when her daughter was small). It wants the girl home, in the two rooms, safe. It fears the empty room and being left alone with a husband who can't work. It thinks: *"Law can be studied in Fès."* It hid the acceptance letter for two days *(dossier: secret)*. It shows up at night, and at the word "October."
- **The Rehearser** (an adult manager). It wants the departure to go cleanly. It fears that if Souad cries at the station, the girl will stay. It thinks: *"Not one tear. Wave, turn, walk."* It rehearses every night *(dossier: home)* and fills the hours with work, including the office shift from 23:00 to 04:00 that pays for the train. The world rewards this part. It is polarised with the Keeper: the harder she rehearses, the louder the Keeper gets.

Beneath both sits an **exile**: the sixteen-year-old in the tannery, working instead of studying, who lost a fingertip there *(inference)*. Her burden is *"girls like us use their hands."* This is a **legacy burden**, and it is what the daughter's train is meant to break.

Souad is the mechanism's quiet double. She is doing the thing Khadija never did. She can see Khadija's fixation from the first room because she has her own Keeper. Her Self is unusually available. It shows as patience and directness, and in a choice about what to keep to herself: she recognises Khadija and says nothing.

### Evidence
- Hid the letter, and is ashamed of it *(dossier: secret)*
- Rehearses not crying *(dossier: home)*
- Lost her fingertip at sixteen in a tannery *(dossier: body)*
- Recognised Khadija and decided not to say so *(dossier: history)*
- "I'm not going anywhere" and the back line *(dossier: speech)*

### Under pressure
Each time Khadija postpones the scrub, Souad waits. She is not hurt by it. She has seen every kind of shyness. When Khadija's following goes too far, the Rehearser hands control to Self, and Souad delivers the line about Khadija's own back. In her own worst five minutes, at the station, the Keeper takes over. Her hand closes on her daughter's sleeve while her mouth says "Go."

### Tells
- She tucks tips into the knot of her headscarf and counts them by touch without looking.
- Her voice fills three rooms, but she drops it to a murmur when anyone mentions her daughter.
- She sets the bucket down and leaves it, waiting with her arms folded, unhurried.
- She lays the hand with the missing fingertip flat on a woman's back, openly, never hiding it.

### Arc
One moment of Self leading is a single concrete action: she presses two dirhams into Khadija's palm and says, "From the finger." She pays a six-year debt and hands over her own secret at once. It costs her the anonymity that let her watch over Khadija. If she does not change, she keeps the debt, keeps the recognition, and keeps the letter in the drawer a little longer each time.

### What my lens can't explain
Why she has left the 2-dirham debt unpaid for six years. It could be a tether, or simple forgetting, and IFS has no way to tell the two apart here.

### Confidence
Medium. The polarisation is written into the sheet, but the exile is my inference from one injury.
