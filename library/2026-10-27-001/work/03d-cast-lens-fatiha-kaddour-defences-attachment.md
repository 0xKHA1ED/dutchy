## Defences-attachment reading of Fatiha Kaddour

### Reading
Fatiha's central defence is **suppression**, and it is mostly mature. She recognised Khadija's walk in the cold room and kept working *(dossier: second secret)*. She heard the cough in 1981 and has said nothing for forty-four years *(secret)*. Around that she has built **altruism shading into rationalisation**: the stolen tin becomes "a wedding present she gave without being asked" *(history)*, so that she was generous rather than robbed. Her attachment is **avoidant, organised through care**. She hands out closeness as orders and orders as closeness *(how they speak)*, and her hands stay busy so nobody has to talk. Her sleeve ritual says *I am at work now*. She performs it even naked in the steam *(habit)*, because work is where feeling cannot reach her.

The mechanism runs backwards in her. She does not dread the double; she identified it on sight. Khadija is the uncanny figure for Fatiha too, the woman who stayed. Fatiha's way through is to scrub that figure, slowly and for free, and not to name her.

### Evidence
- She heard the cough behind "She's out" and has told no one *(secret)*.
- She recognised the flat left foot and stayed silent *(second secret)*.
- The theft was reframed as a gift *(history)*.
- The original shoes are worn only for the Fès journey *(the shoes)*. Leaving is preserved as a sacred object.
- She has eaten from one dish with another Khadija for twenty-eight years *(home)*.

### Under pressure
The trigger is Khadija calling her a thief. Her first move is to push up sleeves that aren't there and keep the glove going at exactly the same pace. Her second is an order: "The other shoulder." If Khadija pulls away, she refills the bucket and offers the water to a stranger.

**Worst five minutes:**
1. She stops scrubbing. This is the only time she ever does.
2. She laughs once, short.
3. She says, "Keep the tin. It was a present." This is generosity used as a blade.
4. She almost says "You were home," and instead pours the bucket over Khadija's back. It is fast, and it is the last kindness.
5. She leaves the steam without dressing her hair. She will be at her brother's flat-agent by noon, because signing is the only reply she allows herself.

Recovery looks like doing more work. She skips no hammam days.

### Tells
- She shoves her forearms up past the elbow before any task, even when bare-armed.
- Her answers to hard questions are instructions: "Turn. Breathe. Other side."
- She makes a dry joke about not being paid today at the moment the conversation turns painful.
- She keeps her eyes on the back she's scrubbing and never looks at the face.
- She carries the buckled flats wrapped in a cloth and puts them on only at the bus door.

### Arc
Change would mean saying the cough aloud and letting the tin be a theft again. That would cost her forty years of having been the generous one, and expose her as someone who was left. If she doesn't change, she signs for the flat, scrubs Khadija three times a week, and the two women grow old a street apart, each the other's unlived life, with nothing ever said.

### What my lens can't explain
I can't account for why she chose, and kept, a second Khadija to share her dish. It could be repetition, repair, or coincidence.

### Confidence
Medium. The defences are clearly documented, but there is no interview to show how she narrates her own story.
