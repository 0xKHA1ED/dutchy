# La Paire (The Pair)

**In one line:** Three weeks from retirement, a night-shift cashier with one hour on the meter tracks a "pickpocket" through a Fès hammam. She leaves before the woman being scrubbed can turn round, because that woman's back looks too much like her own.
**Built on:** The double · crime · comic turning tragic · bitter · hammam rooms (cold, warm, hot) as structure, each chapter ending on a minute-mark
**Setting:** A women's day hammam in the old medina of Fès, present day
**Length:** About 4,000 words, 4 chapters, about 30 minutes of audio
**Level:** B1

## What it was meant to do
The listener first enjoys a sharp, funny cashier hunting a thief. In the hot room they realise she is avoiding a face, not chasing a crime. When she picks the parking ticket over the truth, they share her relief at not looking.
*Deep question:* When we refuse a life, do we spend the rest of ours making sure we never see it lived?

**The cold reader thought it was about:** "A woman who has spent her life keeping accounts and suspecting others watches the love she gave up being lived, gladly, by another woman with her name, her feet and her hip, and she chooses to count minutes rather than turn and be seen, and pays anyway."
**Moment that hit hardest:** "— Khadija. / Deux femmes ont répondu." (Someone says "Khadija." Two women answered.)

## How it got here
- **Round 1 failed Recognition (3/5).** Readers couldn't place the name "Fatiha." The fix tied the thin woman to Khadija's own shoes through her detective's eye, and linked the name to a childhood memory of letters traced on her back.
- **Cut the line spoken to the listener at the climax** ("You too have looked at a clock like that"). Three critics said it broke the spell and explained the moment.
- **Stopped giving away the ticket.** She no longer counts past zero aloud, so the listener hopes until the windscreen. A traffic warden is now planted in chapter 1.
- **Round 2:** the slow opening was trimmed, and a small, uncommented act of kindness was added (she shims a wobbly stool). The thief theory now holds longer, and extra echoes at the exit were removed.
- **The judge chose version C** over A, which had a thinner ending, and B, which over-explained. C lost two lovely details: the "yoghurts past a laser at 3 a.m." joke and the half-erased "3 and half an 8" shoe size.

## Worth a look
- **The earrings flash** ("380. Half. 190.") is still opaque to every cold reader. Check that the ending's thumb-on-earring still moves you without decoding it.
- **Fatiha's identity** is still reached by guesswork. One predictor attached the name to the wrong woman.
- **The level gate** was described as failing, and no pass was recorded. Confirm before publishing.
- **Two coincidences in one morning** (the scrubber and the figure from her past). One critic thought this edges toward contrivance.
