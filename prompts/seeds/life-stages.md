# Life stages

Age matters less than the life moment that comes with it. Each entry pairs an age with a pressure built into that stage of life. The forge treats the age as fixed and the moment as a strong suggestion.

- 17, the last summer before everyone leaves for different cities
- 19, first year living away from home, calling home less than promised
- 22, just graduated, nothing has started, everyone else seems to have started
- 26, the friend group is beginning to marry
- 29, a decade-long relationship that has quietly become a habit
- 31, just became a parent and doesn't recognise themselves
- 34, has the job they wanted at 20 and doesn't want it
- 37, their own parent is starting to forget things
- 39, the last year of their thirties, measured against a list made at 25
- 41, divorced last year, living in a flat with too few forks
- 44, the children are teenagers and no longer need them in the same way
- 46, made redundant after twenty years with one company
- 48, an affair has started and they haven't decided anything
- 50, the age their own father was when he died
- 52, newly divorced, dating for the first time since the 1990s
- 55, a grandchild arrives and changes the family's centre of gravity
- 58, caring for a parent full-time, invisible to everyone
- 60, a health scare has just ended well, and they don't feel lucky
- 63, three weeks before retirement
- 66, first year retired, doesn't know what Tuesdays are for
- 70, a widow or widower for one year
- 73, moving out of the family home into a smaller flat
- 76, has outlived most of their friends
- 78, the last of their generation in the family
- 81, falling in love again, to their children's alarm
- 85, asked to give up their driving licence
