# Changed rules

For speculative stories only. One rule of reality is changed and everything else stays ordinary: rent, bad landlords, family dinners, bus timetables. The ordinariness is what makes the change frightening or funny. Each rule should press directly on a human mechanism.

## Mind and memory

- Memories can be rented out for a weekend
- Everyone can hear what others really think of them for one minute a day
- Dreams are recorded and stored by the state
- You can delete one memory a year, legally
- People can donate their remaining years to someone else
- Grief has to be registered and has an official end date
- Everyone's inner voice is audible in public libraries

## Technology and AI

- An app shows how much each person you know actually likes you, as a number
- Dead people can be kept as voice assistants, trained on their messages
- An AI can apply for jobs on your behalf, and it is better at being you than you are
- Every apology is checked by a machine for sincerity before it is legal
- A city's traffic lights are controlled by an AI that seems to have favourites
- Your phone tells you, once a day, one thing you are lying to yourself about
- Robots can be hired to attend family events in your place
- Smart homes refuse to open the door when they judge you to be in a bad mood

## Bodies and time

- People stop ageing at the age they were happiest, and nobody knows in advance when that will be
- Shadows sometimes stay behind when a person leaves a room
- Everyone is born with a date printed on their wrist; nobody knows what it means
- Sleep can be bought and sold
- People can pause time for themselves for one hour a year
- Every person can visit one place in the past, once

## Society and rules

- Lying is legal one day a year
- Lovers must register with the town hall after the third date
- Every citizen must judge a stranger's life once in their lifetime
- Silence is taxed
- You can sue a friend for emotional neglect
- People can be fired from their families
- Neighbours vote every year on who stays in the street
- Every apartment block elects one person to be blamed for everything

## The uncanny, played straight

- Letters sometimes arrive from the sender's future self
- One floor of every building exists only at night
- Lost things return on the first day of each month, with notes
- Mirrors show the person you would be if you had made the other choice
- Everyone has an identical stranger somewhere in their city, and an agency that finds them
