# Pressures

What squeezes the situation and forces the mechanism to run harder. A pressure gives the story a clock, a cost or a witness.

## Clocks

- The last week before retirement
- The last day before a shop closes for good
- 24 hours before a wedding
- The night before a court date
- A visa that expires on Friday
- The final performance of a play that has run for ten years
- A flight that was cancelled; the next one is in two days
- A deadline for an inheritance condition
- The last ferry of the season
- One hour left on a parking meter
- A building scheduled for demolition at dawn
- A promise that must be kept by midnight

## Tests and judgements

- A driving test, a citizenship test, an exam, an audition
- An inspection that could close the business
- A job interview for a job they're overqualified for
- A home visit from a social worker
- A competition final
- A performance review with a new, younger boss
- A medical result that arrives today

## Visitors and intrusions

- A visitor who won't leave
- A relative arrives unannounced and stays
- A stranger who knows their name
- A new neighbour who is too friendly
- A former partner moves in next door
- A child from a previous relationship appears
- An old friend, now famous, comes back to town

## Debts and secrets

- A debt that comes due
- A secret someone else has just discovered
- A lie told years ago that is about to be checked
- Money found that belongs to someone else
- A promise made to a dying person
- A letter that should have been delivered years ago
- Evidence that could ruin someone they love

## Confinements

- Snowed in with people they avoid
- A strike strands everyone in a station
- A power cut in a lift
- A quarantine in a hotel
- A long car journey with someone they've fallen out with
- A night shift with a colleague they despise
