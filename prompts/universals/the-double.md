# The double and the uncanny

**In one line:** Meeting something deeply familiar that should not exist (a copy of yourself, your own life lived by someone else, a message from yourself) is terrifying because it shows a part of you that you disowned (Freud's "uncanny", Otto Rank's "double").

## How it runs

- **Trigger:** an encounter with a mirror-figure: a person who looks like you, has your name, lives your other life, or knows things only you know.
- **Inner move:** the double carries a disowned possibility: the life not chosen, the self not admitted, the thing done and forgotten.
- **Visible behaviour:** fascination and dread; following, investigating, competing with or trying to destroy the double; a slow loss of certainty about which one is "real".
- **Cost:** the person's sense of identity frays. The only way out is to admit the double's life as one's own possibility.

## From outside

- Obsession with one stranger.
- Repeated mistaken identity.
- The person starts imitating the double, or the double imitates them.
- A growing confusion about what really happened.

## Under pressure

The double moves closer: takes the job, meets the partner, answers the phone. The story escalates in strangeness while staying grounded in ordinary detail. Endings: the person and the double swap; the double turns out to be the person's own doing (letters they wrote and forgot); or the person finally talks to the double and recognises themselves.

## Lenses that see it best

Jungian (shadow, the unlived life), Lacanian (the mirror stage, the imaginary), defences (dissociation), existential (freedom and the life not chosen).

## Story shapes

Wonder or mystery as the surface question; the loop; reversal; unreliable narrator. Often paired with one changed rule for speculative stories.

## Collisions

Return of the repressed, the shadow, persona collapse.

## Seeds

- He gets a letter from himself, posted tomorrow.
- A woman keeps receiving compliments meant for another woman with her name in the same building.
- A retired actor sees a young actor playing him in a film about a life he never lived.

## Traps

- Explaining the mechanics (clones, time travel, twins) in detail. The rule should be simple, and the story is about the person.
- Pure horror with no inner meaning. The double must carry something the protagonist disowned.
- A twist that the double was "only imagined", with nothing gained.
