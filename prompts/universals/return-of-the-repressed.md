# The return of the repressed

**In one line:** What we bury comes back, disguised, at the worst possible moment (Freud).

## How it runs

- **Trigger:** an event, a memory or a part of the self is too painful or shameful to keep in awareness.
- **Inner move:** it is pushed out of mind and the person builds a life that never touches it.
- **Visible behaviour:** avoidance of certain places, words or people; then, later, strange symptoms: slips of the tongue, a recurring dream, an accent coming back, a habit from childhood, uncanny coincidences that are really the person's own doing.
- **Cost:** the energy spent not-knowing; and when it returns, it returns sideways and out of control.

## From outside

- A topic the person changes too quickly.
- A place they will drive an hour to avoid.
- Something from the past leaking into the present: an old song hummed without noticing, an old name used by mistake.
- An overreaction to something that seems trivial to everyone else.

## Under pressure

The story brings the buried thing closer: a person from the past, a return to a place, an anniversary, a document. The repressed comes back first in disguise (a symptom, a stranger who seems familiar, a mistake). Endings: the person finally looks at it; the person re-buries it at a cost the listener can see; or it turns out others always knew.

## Lenses that see it best

Defences (repression, denial, dissociation), IFS (exiles), Jungian (complexes that act autonomously), Lacanian (the symptom as a message).

## Story shapes

Mystery as the surface question; unreliable narrator; the loop; reversal.

## Collisions

The double, projection, the fetish as symbol, the protector that harms.

## Seeds

- A man who erased his hometown accent starts hearing it in his own voicemail greetings.
- A woman who never talks about her brother keeps setting an extra place at the table without noticing.
- An interpreter starts mistranslating one word, always the same one, in every meeting.

## Traps

- Trauma as the default content of the repressed. It can be a love, an ambition, a joy, a crime, an embarrassing act.
- The flashback that explains everything. The return should be felt before it is understood.
- Psychobabble: no therapist characters explaining "repression".
